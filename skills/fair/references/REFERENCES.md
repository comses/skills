# FAIR Core Literature

Canonical bibliography for the FAIR Research Objects skill. Cite these sources by short form (e.g. "Wilkinson et al. 2016") elsewhere in this skill; keep the full citation, DOI, and usage note here as the single source of truth.

## FAIR Principles

**Wilkinson, M. D., Dumontier, M., Aalbersberg, I. J., et al. (2016).** The FAIR Guiding Principles for scientific data management and stewardship. *Scientific Data*, 3, 160018.
DOI: [10.1038/sdata.2016.18](https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2016.18)
Used for: the foundational F/A/I/R definitions this skill applies to every research object type.

## FAIR Research Software (FAIR4RS)

**Chue Hong, N. P., Katz, D. S., Barker, M., et al. (2022).** FAIR Principles for Research Software (FAIR4RS Principles) 1.0. *Research Data Alliance Recommendations and Outputs*.
DOI: [10.15497/RDA00068](https://doi.org/10.15497/RDA00068)
Used for: the normative FAIR4RS principles text itself.

## Research Software Engineering

**Wilson, G., Aruliah, D. A., Brown, C. T., et al. (2014).** Best Practices for Scientific Computing. *PLOS Biology*, 12(1), e1001745.
DOI: [10.1371/journal.pbio.1001745](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.1001745)
Used for: foundational scientific software development practices (version control, testing, modularity).

**Wilson, G., Bryan, J., Cranston, K., Kitzes, J., Nederbragt, L., & Teal, T. K. (2017).** Good enough practices in scientific computing. *PLOS Computational Biology*, 13(6), e1005510.  
DOI: [10.1371/journal.pcbi.1005510](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1005510)  
Used for: pragmatic, low-overhead scientific computing workflows (project organization, version control, data management, collaboration, manuscript preparation, and reproducibility) appropriate for individual researchers and small teams.

**Jiménez, R. C., Kuzak, M., Alhamdoosh, M., et al. (2017).** Four simple recommendations to encourage best practices in research software. *F1000Research*, 6, 876.
DOI: [10.12688/f1000research.11407.1](https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.11407.1)
Used for: lightweight, low-barrier adoption recommendations (license, citation, testing, documentation).

## Computational Models

**Grimm, V., Railsback, S. F., Vincenot, C. E., et al. (2020).** The ODD Protocol for Describing Agent-Based and Other Simulation Models: A Second Update to Improve Clarity, Replication, and Structural Realism. *Journal of Artificial Societies and Social Simulation*, 23(2), 7.
DOI: [10.18564/jasss.4259](https://doi.org/10.18564/jasss.4259)
Used for: the current ODD protocol for describing agent-based and other simulation models.

**Grimm, V., Augusiak, J., Focks, A., et al. (2014).** Towards better modelling and decision support: Documenting model development, testing, and analysis using TRACE. *Ecological Modelling*, 280, 129–139.
DOI: [10.1016/j.ecolmodel.2014.01.018](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecolmodel.2014.01.018)
Used for: the TRACE framework for documenting model design, testing, and evaludation.

**Open Modeling Foundation (OMF).** Living community standards and metadata for computational and simulation models.
URL: https://www.openmodelingfoundation.org
Used for: OMF metadata and community modeling standards; no single canonical citation — treat as a living reference, not a fixed paper.

## Data Stewardship

**DataCite.** Metadata schema and infrastructure for dataset persistent identifiers. Current version is 4.7
URL: https://datacite-metadata-schema.readthedocs.io/
Used for: canonical dataset metadata; a living schema rather than a single citable paper. Cite the specific DataCite Metadata Schema version in use where precision matters.

**Soiland-Reyes, S., Sefton, P., Crosas, M., et al. (2022).** Packaging research artefacts with RO-Crate. *Data Science*, 5(2), 97–138.
DOI: [10.3233/DS-210053](https://doi.org/10.3233/DS-210053)
Used for: the RO-Crate specification for packaging research objects and their metadata.

## Software Management Planning

**EVERSE RSQKit.** Community guidance on software management planning.
URL: https://everse.software/RSQKit/software_management_planning
Used for: the RSE-process content (governance, testing, sustainability) in `assets/SMP-TEMPLATE.md`; a living guidance resource rather than a single citable paper.

## Agent Skill Stewardship

**The GW Open Source Program Office (2026).** Skills are infrastructure, and a new open-source artifact class.
